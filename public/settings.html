Permissions are granted to "Kiki Agent" (the part that actually does the work), which is also the name shown in System Settings. Apple requires you to flip the switches yourself; Full Disk Access has no prompt, so "Open" opens the relevant System Settings page and reveals "Kiki Agent" in Finder — drag it into the list, then turn the switch on. All of this happens on the Mac running Kiki (the remote one when connected remotely).
